In 1872, sailors boarded a ship in the middle of the Atlantic. The ship was in perfect order. The crew was gone.
The Mary Celeste had left New York a month earlier with ten people aboard, including the captain's wife and their little girl.
The cargo was untouched. The food and water were untouched. Only the lifeboat was missing.
So ten people climbed into a tiny open boat and left a working ship behind. Nobody has ever found them.
